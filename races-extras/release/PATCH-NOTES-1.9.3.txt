CoA Custom 1.9.3 (x64 client, test build)
=========================================
NEW
- x64 client CoA64.exe (built from the CoA64 decomp, https://github.com/Westen-CoACore/CoA64) with the Retail race adapter:
  Worgen (Wolf / Human) and Dracthyr (Dragon / Visage) use the real Retail models, read from Blizzard's CDN.
- Per-form customization in the creator (tabs per form, BOTH shows the other form behind).
- The creation / selection screens shrink oversized races (Dracthyr, Kyrian, Venthyr, Centaur, Faceless One, Vrykul).
- Server: the saved Retail look and the second form are stored per character (character_retail_look, character_form_look,
  character_body_shape) and sent to the client. Worgen get Two Forms (spell 110690) to switch form; Dracthyr use Visage.
- Chosen Identity removed from Dracthyr.
- Fix: a texture with a bad format no longer crashes the client.

KNOWN ISSUES (test build)
- Form switching in the world and the saved look of the second form are still being worked on.
- Some races can still be too large on the creation / selection screens.
- Barber shop, customize and faction change do not save the Retail look yet (creation does).
- Saberon bots: white armor. Alpha Worgen has no Retail mapping yet.
