CoA Custom 1.9.4 (x64 client, test build)
=========================================
Complete package: this release alone is enough (Server + Client-1 .. Client-9). Nothing from 1.9.2 / 1.9.3 is needed.

FIXED
- Worgen "Two Forms" and Dracthyr "Visage" now switch form in the world. The look / form / body-shape packets used opcodes
  the client dropped; they are now 0x575 - 0x577 (client and server both changed).
- Dracthyr / Worgen keep their creator customisation and the Dracthyr armour comes back (same cause).
- Clicking a Worgen or Dracthyr on the character screen no longer corrupts the heap (a raw 32-bit resize command ran on the
  selected character; the stage scale is now applied natively for every race).
- Oversized races (Centaur, Vrykul, Faceless One, Dracthyr, Venthyr, Kyrian) are scaled to fit the creator, the character
  screen and the enter-world screen, without zooming the camera.
- Tuskarr male: the model had animation tracks that pointed past its .anim files and crashed the client. The earlier model is back
  (patch-ZZZZE.MPQ); the sheathing animation of that rebuild is gone.
- Ranged weapons no longer need ammo on the client either (the server already ignored it): Starcaller and every other class can shoot.
- Body sliders: closing the in-world Body panel without Save restores the saved sliders (the one-second auto-save is gone);
  leaving the creator drops them; a character created with sliders keeps them.
- The client no longer crashes on exit (archive close guarded) and ignores textures with an invalid format.

NEW
- No more "Retail: acquiring missing data": every Retail file the Worgen and Dracthyr need (both forms, every customisation
  choice, their animations) is shipped in Client-7 .. Client-9 (Data\Retail\Cache). No download from Blizzard's CDN for these races.
- Tool: CdnAssetDownload (Tools/RetailAssets) prefetches a retail race's files into that cache.
- Tool in the repo (not shipped): tools/crashcatch.cpp runs the game under a debugger and writes the stack of a crash to CrashCatch.txt.

KNOWN ISSUES (test build)
- The client can still crash sometimes in the creator (heap corruption); CrashCatch is there to find it.
- Barber shop, customize and faction change do not save the Retail look yet (creation does).
- Saberon bots: white armor. Alpha Worgen has no Retail mapping yet. Arakkoa male look is being checked.
