Subclass races (stefanw1337) - changes since 1.9.6 - 2026-11-02
================================================================
Server: build from stefanw1337/azerothcore-wotlk-coa, branch ilusixn-195-server (c1d22382), or see README.md here.
Client: the models and tables below are in our patch-ZZZZY.MPQ (the only client patch of ours from now on).

WITCH HUNTER VAULT
- Vault no longer plays a plain jump. Each original race vaults its own way: handsprings (Human, Troll, Draenei),
  three small springs (Gnome), a flip without hands (Night Elf), side flips (Undead, Blood Elf), and a dive roll that
  ends in a shoulder charge (Tauren, Orc, Dwarf). Backing up flips backwards. Distance and speed are unchanged.
- Two-Headed Ogre: she leaps, lands on her butt, bounces off it and lands on her feet.
- The Twins: the brother bends over right in front of you and you leapfrog over his back, then carry on to the end of
  the Vault. If he rides piggyback he hops off first. Works with Witch Hunter as main class or subclass.

THE TWINS
- Hair under hats: short, half-long and long hair stay as chosen with only the top cut away; bald stays bald. The
  brother's hat does the same for him.
- The brother's forms follow the spell rules: taking one pays its cost and starts its cooldown, and timed forms such as
  Accursed Form end when their time runs out. A brother at range no longer swings his melee weapon.
- Play easy, cowboy: the brother leaves a spent timed form when none of its rage spenders or builders can be used.
  Tank forms are kept.
- Melee-and-caster specializations keep the brother at range; in a Bloodmage cursed form he wears the incarnation chosen
  in the wardrobe and fights up close.
- Your own mana bar no longer flickers while a shared mana pool fills up.

TWO-HEADED OGRE
- Her cast now holds a bent arm up with the fist before the face on both heads, instead of a straight arm thrown
  forward.

SUBCLASSING
- The subclass name shows only on the Character tab of the character window, not on Pets, Reputation, Skills or
  Currency.
- Dual Wield shows once in the spellbook, whichever class teaches it.
- The subclass addon learns the race from the server, so the twin's wardrobe button, the Two-Thins tab and the Ogre's
  looks keep working under any race name (such as "The Twins").

TOOLS
- tools/mkmergepatch.py merges our client patches into one patch-ZZZZY.MPQ (plain Python, runs on Windows).
- tools/mkvault.py builds the Ogre and Twins Vault into their models and adds the animation and visual kit rows.
